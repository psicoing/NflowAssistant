---
name: Voice commercial positioning
description: Product boundary for NUXA phone calls versus the NUXA.life app.
---

NUXA phone calls are informational and commercial: they explain NUXA.life and help interested companies understand how to contract it. Psychological support is provided by the app, not by the phone assistant.

**Why:** The phone experience should encourage company adoption without creating the expectation of psychological care or therapy by telephone.

**How to apply:** Introduce the voice assistant as an AI information assistant, focus conversations on the service and next commercial step, and redirect psychological-support requests to the app or appropriate professional/emergency services. Repeat the product boundary and core NUXA facts in response-level Realtime instructions, not only at session level; otherwise later turns can drift into generic wellness or fitness advice.
