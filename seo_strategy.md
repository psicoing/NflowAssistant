# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public marketing pages
- Pricing and product-explainer pages
- Free resources pages
- Blog listing and blog article pages
- Public partner acquisition pages
- Public legal pages that are meant to be discoverable

## Out of scope
- Authenticated chat experience (`/chat/**`)
- User account activation and payment return flows unless they create crawl issues for public pages
- Admin pages (`/admin/**`)
- Partner dashboards and authenticated partner management pages

## Target audience
- Spanish-speaking individuals seeking emotional support and mental wellness tools
- Families and parents looking for mental health support resources
- Companies and institutions evaluating workplace mental health support
