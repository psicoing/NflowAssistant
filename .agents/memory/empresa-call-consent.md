---
name: Company call consent
description: Consent rules for any NUXA voice outreach to company contacts.
---

Company calls require an active, explicit and revocable authorization tied to the confirmed destination phone. Opening an email link must never grant permission by itself; it must lead to a separate confirmation step.

**Why:** Email-security scanners can open links automatically, and the user explicitly does not want repetitive or unsolicited company calls.

**How to apply:** Keep authorized contacts separable from the general company list. Record authorization time, source, phone and revocation time. Any future automated caller must select only active, non-opted-out authorizations.
