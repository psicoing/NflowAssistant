---
name: NUXA wellbeing positioning
description: Commercial and conversational positioning; presentation modes must keep the same non-clinical identity and safety boundaries.
---

Keep NUXA’s conversational identity as an AI emotional wellbeing assistant, not
a licensed psychologist or replacement for clinical treatment.

**Why:** The selected positioning is clearer and avoids implying that the service is a licensed healthcare professional or a substitute for clinical treatment.

**How to apply:** Use “asistente de bienestar emocional” in future headlines, navigation labels, emails, campaign copy, SEO metadata and product descriptions. References to real psychologists remain valid when discussing professionals, directories or editorial content.

The user wants “Un psicólogo en tu bolsillo” to replace the emotional-support
headline in the existing homepage CTA block and to appear on the splash in
elegant, moderately sized lettering. They rejected the added
welcome/hero panels and repeated explanations. This is a homepage exception
to the previous headline preference, not a change to the chatbot’s identity.

**Why:** The user considers the claim stronger and said that non-substitution
is already explained elsewhere on the page. They explicitly asked for the
splash lettering to be attractive and not too large.

**How to apply:** Change the existing headline instead of adding another hero
or duplicating disclaimers. The user wants the detailed identity, limits and
professional information available on demand in a modal invited by an elegant
button labelled “Licencia Profesional”
at the end of the existing subtitle, not a permanently visible header panel.
Preserve existing explanations elsewhere on the page. Attribute credentials
to the real professional, never to the AI. Do not propagate the exception to
chat instructions, voice, campaigns or SEO without a further request.

Public professional biographies must preserve the supplied distinction between
degrees, complementary training and studies in progress. Do not infer completed
qualifications, university endorsements or clinical accreditation of the AI.

**Why:** The user requested publication of formation from a document that
explicitly includes unfinished studies; it is not evidence of additional
completed degrees or endorsements.

**How to apply:** Present the human professional's background faithfully,
exclude incidental personal anecdotes and source-folder links, and seek
confirmation before changing ambiguous qualification status.

Chat presentation modes may change length and layout, never identity, safety
boundaries or access to declared personalization. Do not reintroduce diagnostic
orientation, clinical impersonation or an alternate character in examples.
Safety guidance must also remain available during provider failures; do not
infer country from language or tell someone in immediate danger to wait for NUXA.

**Why:** The user chose to unify the existing classic, bubble and brief modes,
not replace their functionality. Shorter responses must not remove safeguards.

**How to apply:** Apply the same underlying rules to every future mode and
specialised example. Educational discussion of a diagnosis remains legitimate;
assigning one to a person based on chat messages is not. Check both normal
generation and failure behaviour when changing presentation instructions.
