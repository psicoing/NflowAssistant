---
name: NUXA wellbeing positioning
description: Commercial and conversational positioning; presentation modes must keep the same non-clinical identity and safety boundaries.
---

Present NUXA as “Tu asistente de bienestar emocional, disponible 24/7”. Avoid using “tu psicólogo IA”, “psicólogo digital” or equivalent claims as NUXA’s commercial identity.

**Why:** The selected positioning is clearer and avoids implying that the service is a licensed healthcare professional or a substitute for clinical treatment.

**How to apply:** Use “asistente de bienestar emocional” in future headlines, navigation labels, emails, campaign copy, SEO metadata and product descriptions. References to real psychologists remain valid when discussing professionals, directories or editorial content.

Chat presentation modes may change length and layout, never identity, safety
boundaries or access to declared personalization. Do not reintroduce diagnostic
orientation, clinical impersonation or an alternate character in examples.
Safety guidance must also remain available during provider failures; do not
infer country from language or tell someone in immediate danger to wait for NUXA.

**Why:** The user chose to unify the existing classic, bubble and brief modes,
not replace their functionality. Shorter responses must not remove safeguards.

**How to apply:** Apply the same underlying rules to every future mode and
specialised example. Educational discussion of a diagnosis remains legitimate;
assigning one to a person based on chat messages is not. Check both normal
generation and failure behaviour when changing presentation instructions.
