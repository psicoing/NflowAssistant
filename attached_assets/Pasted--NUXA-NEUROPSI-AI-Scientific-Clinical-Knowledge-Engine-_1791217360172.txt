# NUXA — NEUROPSI-AI
## Scientific & Clinical Knowledge Engine Upgrade

We want to substantially improve the AI chat engine used by NUXA.

The objective is NOT simply to add links to psychology journals.

The objective is to evolve NEUROPSI-AI into a structured, evidence-informed psychological and neuropsychological reasoning system capable of retrieving, ranking and using high-quality scientific information when answering users.

NUXA should continue to communicate in accessible, empathetic language, but its internal reasoning should increasingly be supported by professional psychological, neuropsychological, neurological and psychosocial literature.

## 1. CREATE A NEW SCIENTIFIC KNOWLEDGE LAYER

Create a dedicated knowledge layer:

**NEUROPSI Scientific Knowledge Base**

It should complement the existing NEUROPSI-AI system rather than replace it.

Organise sources into domains such as:

- Clinical Psychology
- Psychopathology
- Neuropsychology
- Neurology
- Child and Adolescent Psychology
- Educational Psychology
- Social Psychology
- Work and Organisational Psychology
- Legal and Forensic Psychology
- Psychotherapy
- Psychoanalysis
- Addiction and Substance Use
- Social Intervention
- Mental Health
- Cognitive Sciences

## 4. DO NOT TRAIN THE MODEL DIRECTLY ON EVERYTHING

Do not simply insert thousands of articles into the system prompt.

Implement a retrieval architecture.

Preferred pipeline:

USER MESSAGE

↓

NUXA conversational analysis

↓

NEUROPSI-AI clinical concept extraction

↓

Query classification

↓

Scientific retrieval when relevant

↓

Evidence ranking

↓

Relevant-document selection

↓

Context construction

↓

LLM reasoning

↓

Safety / clinical validation layer

↓

USER RESPONSE

The scientific system should therefore work primarily as a RAG-style knowledge system rather than enormously increasing the permanent prompt.

## 5. QUERY CLASSIFICATION

Before retrieving literature, determine what the user is asking about.

Examples:

"I can't stop thinking about things at night."

Possible concepts:

- rumination
- anxiety
- insomnia
- cognitive arousal
- stress

The system should internally transform the conversational message into appropriate professional concepts before searching the scientific knowledge base.

Another example:

"My 8-year-old cannot concentrate at school."

Potential concepts:

- attention
- ADHD
- executive functions
- learning disorders
- anxiety
- sleep
- developmental factors
- educational environment

The AI must NOT immediately conclude that the child has ADHD.

It should reason through possible explanations and ask useful questions.

## 6. EVIDENCE HIERARCHY

NEUROPSI-AI must distinguish between levels and types of evidence.

Suggested ranking:

1. Clinical guidelines and authoritative health guidance
2. Systematic reviews / meta-analyses
3. High-quality peer-reviewed research
4. Professional psychological journals
5. Academic books and reference works
6. Professional consensus documents
7. Other academically relevant literature

The ranking should also consider:

- publication date
- methodological quality
- relevance to the question
- population studied
- sample size when available
- replication
- consistency with other evidence

Do NOT treat every published article as equally reliable.

## 7. MULTIPLE PSYCHOLOGICAL PERSPECTIVES

NUXA should not become ideologically tied to one psychological school.

When appropriate, NEUROPSI-AI may consider:

- cognitive-behavioural approaches
- behavioural psychology
- neuropsychology
- cognitive neuroscience
- systemic approaches
- developmental psychology
- humanistic approaches
- psychodynamic approaches
- psychoanalytic literature
- social psychology

However, distinguish clearly between empirical evidence, theoretical interpretation and clinical hypothesis.

## 8. NEUROPSYCHOLOGICAL REASONING

Strengthen NEUROPSI-AI's ability to recognise concepts related to:

- attention
- memory
- executive functions
- language
- processing speed
- visuospatial functions
- social cognition
- emotional regulation
- cognitive development
- ageing
- acquired brain injury
- neurodevelopment

The system must distinguish between:

**symptom → hypothesis → assessment → diagnosis**

A symptom is not a diagnosis.

## 9. CLINICAL DIFFERENTIAL REASONING

When relevant, NEUROPSI-AI should internally consider alternative explanations.

For example:

Difficulty concentrating could potentially be associated with:

ADHD  
anxiety  
depression  
sleep deprivation  
stress  
medication  
substance use  
medical conditions  
environmental factors  
learning difficulties

The user should receive a clear explanation rather than an enormous differential diagnosis.

Internal reasoning can be complex.

External communication should remain simple.

## 10. RESPONSE STYLE

Do NOT turn NUXA into an academic search engine.

The user should not normally receive a response full of citations and technical terminology.

NUXA should translate scientific knowledge into understandable language.

For example:

Instead of:

"Persistent cognitive arousal and maladaptive repetitive negative thinking are associated with sleep-onset latency."

NUXA could say:

"What you're describing can happen when the brain remains mentally activated even though the body is tired. Worry and repetitive thoughts can make it difficult to switch into sleep."

Scientific sophistication should happen primarily **behind the conversation**.

## 11. OPTIONAL "SCIENTIFIC BASIS" FEATURE

Add an optional UI element beneath appropriate answers:

**📚 Scientific basis**

When opened, it could display:

- relevant scientific concept
- 2–5 selected references
- publication
- year
- DOI or source URL when available
- short explanation of why the source is relevant

This should NOT overwhelm normal users.

URL

This will allow NEUROPSI-AI to know **where its scientific information came from**.

## 13. LANGUAGE

Scientific retrieval should be multilingual.

At minimum:

Spanish  
English  
French  
Catalan

A Spanish question may retrieve an English scientific article.

The evidence should then be synthesised naturally in the user's language.

## 14. SAFETY

Scientific retrieval must never override NUXA's existing safety mechanisms.

Maintain specific handling for:

- suicidal ideation
- self-harm
- violence
- abuse
- medical emergencies
- severe psychiatric symptoms
- minors
- medication questions

NUXA should never present itself as replacing professional diagnosis or emergency services.

## 15. PERFORMANCE

Scientific retrieval should NOT run unnecessarily for every conversational turn.

Use it when the question benefits from scientific support.

Simple conversational interactions can remain fast.

Suggested logic:

general conversation → normal NUXA response

psychological question → NEUROPSI reasoning

complex clinical question → NEUROPSI + scientific retrieval

high-risk situation → safety protocol first + NEUROPSI support

## 18. IMPORTANT COPYRIGHT RULE

NUXA must NEVER assume that being able to see a bibliographic resource means that its full text can legally be copied or indexed.

For restricted resources such as institutional databases or professional-member libraries:

Use only authorised access.

Never bypass login systems.

Never circumvent paywalls.

Never mass-download copyrighted publications without appropriate rights.

Public metadata, abstracts and open-access documents can be treated separately according to their licences and terms.

The final architecture should progressively transform:

**NUXA Chat**

into:

**NUXA + NEUROPSI-AI + Scientific Evidence Engine**

The ideal experience is:

A person writes naturally about what is happening to them.

NUXA understands the language.

NEUROPSI identifies the relevant psychological and neuropsychological concepts.
